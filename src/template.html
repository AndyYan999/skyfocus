A flight-shaped Pomodoro. Pick how long you want to focus, pick somewhere the plane can reach in that time, and the session runs as a flight on a real map.

PASSENGER & TYPES

Set your name once under SESSION TYPE on the pass screen. Session type shows on the pass, in the logbook and in the pilot's calls.

THE FLIGHT

A white aircraft follows the real great-circle route on a world map. Altitude, ground speed, distance remaining and the ETA are all derived from progress through the session. The clock only runs while airborne.

CONTROLS

Pause (space) · +5 min (D) · reset (R) · divert (Esc) · re-fit map (F). Drag the map to pan, wheel to zoom.

TIME-LAPSE

Real flying takes too long to sit through, so the clock is compressed. The default is ×30: every minute of focus stands for 30 minutes in the air, so an hour covers a sixteen-hour leg.
